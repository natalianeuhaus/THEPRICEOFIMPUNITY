ABOUT THE INVESTIGATION

C = M + D − A

CORRUPTION = MONOPOLY + DISCRETION − ACCOUNTABILITY

Robert Klitgaard, 1988

Illegal gold mining, sex trafficking, and public corruption are often investigated as separate crimes. In Madre de Dios, Peru, this project examines how they may operate within interconnected systems of economic exploitation and state protection.

Klitgaard's equation provides a framework: corruption becomes more likely when public officials exercise concentrated authority and broad discretion with insufficient accountability.

Using satellite imagery, judicial records, government documents, and investigative reporting from 2019 to 2025, this project will examine where documented mining activity, trafficking cases, and official misconduct intersect.

The investigation asks whether failures of enforcement reflect limited state resources or deliberate interference, including allegations of bribery, advance warnings of police operations, and manipulation of official records.

Geographic proximity does not establish complicity. Every proposed connection must be supported by documentary evidence. Allegations, disciplinary findings, and criminal convictions will be distinguished.
