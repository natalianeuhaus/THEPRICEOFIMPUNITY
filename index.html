ABOUT THE INVESTIGATION

C = M + D − A
CORRUPTION = MONOPOLY + DISCRETION − ACCOUNTABILITY
Robert Klitgaard, 1988

Illegal gold mining, sex trafficking, and public corruption are often investigated as separate crimes. In Madre de Dios, Peru, this project examines how they may operate within interconnected systems of economic exploitation and state protection.

Klitgaard's equation provides the framework for this investigation: corruption becomes more likely when public officials exercise concentrated authority and broad discretion with insufficient accountability.

Using satellite analysis, judicial records, government documents and investigative reporting, this map examines where documented mining activity, trafficking cases and allegations or findings of official misconduct intersect between 2019 and 2025.

The investigation tests whether failures of enforcement reflect limited state resources or deliberate interference, including alleged bribery, advance warnings of operations and manipulation of official records.

Methodology: Markers and the dashed study-area circle currently represent approximate named areas or city centers. They are not incident-level coordinates, verified mining polygons or evidence of a causal relationship. Dates refer to publication or documented judicial actions, not necessarily the underlying event. Allegations, pretrial detention, disciplinary outcomes and convictions must be distinguished. True mining-footprint polygons will be added only when verified geospatial source files are available. The study-area circle is not a mining footprint.
