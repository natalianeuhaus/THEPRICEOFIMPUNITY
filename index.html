ABOUT THE INVESTIGATION

An evidence-based investigation of mining activity, human trafficking, exploitation and institutional accountability in Madre de Dios. We distinguish unproven allegations, rescue operations, disciplinary sanctions and convictions, and do not equate geographic proximity with criminal involvement.

Method: The 2015–2017 location counts represent presumed trafficking victims across purposes; individual public case records from 2017–2025 are separate. The original 21 police reports used by OjoPúblico have not been obtained as public scans. Gold contours are an automated, generalized regional mining-scar layer (with a 21-polygon La Pampa fallback if the regional dataset is unavailable). Earth Genome notes that segmentation may overestimate footprints and cannot by itself establish illegality. Locations are approximate; no private survivor addresses are shown.

Robert Klitgaard’s framework: C = M + D − A, or corruption risk associated with monopoly, discretion and reduced accountability. The investigation tests explanations rather than presuming corruption.
